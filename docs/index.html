Network-wide DNS ad-blocker on a $2 ESP32-C3 — 500,000 domains (StevenBlack + HaGeZi Multi), RAM Checkpoint Index, In-Memory DNS Cache, Whitelist & Live Log. Flash directly from your browser.

Your browser can't flash — use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. You need to allow the serial port.
Chrome / Edge · desktop ~45 seconds 500k blocklist included
What happens
  1. Click Connect & Install 500k PRO and pick your ESP32-C3's serial port.
  2. It flashes the firmware + a 500,000-domain blocklist (~45s).
  3. After it reboots, it makes a WiFi network called C3-AdBlock-XXXX — join it from your phone, pick your WiFi, type the password.
  4. Plug it into your router's USB port and set it as your router's DNS. Dashboard at c3adblock.local.

No WiFi credentials are baked into this build — you set them on-device, privately.
